# Data Driven Analysis of Pakistan's Education Sector (SDG4)
# 📚 Analysis Years (2010–2023)

## 🎯 Objective

This project provides an in-depth exploration of Pakistan’s education landscape over 12 years, focusing on trends, disparities, and the relationship between infrastructure and outcomes — all tied to Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education.

---

## 🧠 Concepts & Techniques Used

- **Descriptive Statistics**
- **Inferential Statistics**
- **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**
- **Time Series Analysis**
- **Correlation & Covariance**
- **Chi-square Hypothesis Testing**
- **Transition Ratios (e.g., Primary → Middle)**
- **Gender Parity Index (GPI)**
- **Data Visualization**
- **Linear Regression Analysis**
- **Categorical Data Analysis**
---

## 🔍 Key Insights (Concise)

| Category | Insight |
|---------|---------|
| 📈 Growth Trends | Steady increase across most education levels. |
| 🎓 Higher Education | More volatile growth, especially around 2020. |
| 🏫 Infrastructure | Institute and enrollment growth strongly correlated. |
| ⚠️ External Impact | Major dip observed around 2020, likely due to COVID-19. |
| 📊 Recovery | Post-2020 years show signs of stabilization and recovery. |
| 🧑‍🎓 Gender Trends | GPI improving in urban areas; rural gender gap persists. |
| 🚸 Dropouts | Significant decline in enrollment after middle school, especially for girls. |
| 💧 Basic Facilities | Many schools lack clean water, electricity, and sanitation. |
| 🔁 Transition Ratios | Primary-to-middle school transitions more stable than higher ed transitions. |
| 🗺 Provincial Disparities | Urban provinces outperform in both access and literacy. |
